Privacy Policy

Tearz (com.tearz.app) · Effective: 1 August 2026

This policy describes how Tearz (“we”) handles information when you use the Tearz mobile app and related API.

Information we collect

  • Account: email, display name, preferred language, password hash (stored on our servers).
  • Learning content: chat messages with the teacher/companion, vocabulary entries, lesson progress you create in the app.
  • Device / local: streaks, coins, XP, and similar engagement stats may be stored on your device (AsyncStorage). Optional avatar photo stays on device only.
  • Technical: basic request logs needed to operate and secure the API (e.g. timestamps, error codes). We do not sell personal data.

How we use information

  • Authenticate you and deliver email sign-in codes (via Resend or similar).
  • Provide AI teacher / companion responses (processed via our API and language-model providers such as OpenAI).
  • Improve reliability and prevent abuse.

Third parties

We use processors to run the service (hosting, email delivery, AI inference). They process data only to provide those services under their own terms and DPAs where applicable.

Retention & deletion

Account and learning data are kept while your account is active. Contact us to request deletion of server-side account data. Uninstalling the app removes on-device engagement data.

Children

Tearz is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Do not use the app if you are under that age.

Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@tearz.app

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Effective” date above will change. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.
